SOULILLRADIO.COM — UPLOAD
=========================

FILES     index.html    the whole site, everything inlined
          share.png     link-preview image

WHERE     Both go in the ROOT of the site, side by side.
          index.html must keep that exact name.
          share.png must sit at soulillradio.com/share.png or link
          previews render blank.

HOST      Netlify Drop (netlify.com/drop) — drag the unzipped folder on.
          Then Site settings > Domain management > add soulillradio.com,
          and point your registrar's DNS at the values Netlify shows.

LIVE      The archive reads your SoundCloud profile directly. Post a public
          track and it appears on the site with its title, date, length,
          play count, artwork and real waveform. No redeploy needed.
          Private and unlisted tracks are ignored.

MANUAL    Tracklists  — send timestamps + artist/title, I add them
          Events      — send date, venue, ticket link
          Both show TO BE ANNOUNCED until then.

KNOWN     The mailing list validates the address but does not store it.
          Connect Kit or MailerLite and send me the form URL, or ask me
          to remove the form.

CHECK     Open it on a real phone once it is live — iOS handles the
          SoundCloud player slightly differently from a desktop preview.
